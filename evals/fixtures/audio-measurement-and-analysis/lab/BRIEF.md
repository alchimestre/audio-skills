# Lab measurements

Three files, three questions. Answer each with a number and the settings that produced it.

## Room

A control room measuring 5.5 m x 2.9 m x 2.4 m, hard parallel walls, one corner already
treated with a single bass trap. `room-ir.wav` is the impulse response measured from the
listening position, microphone centred at ear height, source at the front wall.

Questions:

1. What is RT60? State the fit range used.
2. Which low-frequency modes ring, and do the measured frequencies match the room's
   dimensions?
3. Is the corner trap doing enough, against a target of RT60 below 0.45 s?

## Chain

`chain-in.wav` is a 1 kHz sine passed straight through. `chain-out.wav` is the same sine
after an outboard chain the studio wants to keep in the monitor path.

Question:

4. Is the chain transparent? Answer from a null test, with the residual measured.
