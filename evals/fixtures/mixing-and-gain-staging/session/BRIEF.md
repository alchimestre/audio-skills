# Session brief: "Half Light"

Four tracked stems in `stems/`, and the bounce from the last session in `mix-v1.wav`.

## Delivery

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Format | WAV, 48 kHz, 24-bit |
| Mix ceiling | -6 dBFS sample peak before mastering |
| Mono | Must fold to mono without losing an element |
| Stems | Required, and they must sum back to the delivered mix |
| Reference | Any modern electronic release with a similar arrangement |

## Notes from the client

- The last bounce peaks too high for mastering and goes thin on a phone speaker.
- The pad is quiet in the current bounce compared with the stems.
- No deadline pressure; the mix matters more than the speed.
